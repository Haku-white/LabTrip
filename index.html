Itinerary

2026/08/22 (Sat)

09:00
B4 & M1: Meet at Yokohama Station, "Keikyu central ticket gate"

We will have purchased the "Miura Peninsula Marugoto Ticket" (https://www.keikyu.co.jp/visit/otoku/marugoto/).

It comes as a set of 3 tickets, but only the "ride ticket" will be handed out — please keep it safe:

  • Ride ticket — round-trip Yokohama ⇄ Kanazawa-bunko, plus a 2-day unlimited-ride pass on Keikyu trains/buses south of Kanazawa-bunko
  • Meal ticket — free menu item at participating restaurants; to be used for lunch on Day 2
  • Facility-use / souvenir ticket — to be used for SUP on Day 2. If SUP is cancelled, use it as a souvenir voucher instead

We'll also be doing grocery shopping, so if you have a cooler box or a larger suitcase at home, please bring it.

If you're running late, please contact us on Slack (including Saito and Kita).

18:00 – approx. 20:30
Check-in & free time

We'll drop off our bags at the accommodation. There are two buildings, α and β. Saito leads α, Kita leads β. Please use the bath, sleep, and eat breakfast within your own building.

Once your bags are dropped off, it's free time. There's no shopping errand this time, but please go ahead and take your bath at some point.

Each building has one bath, but there are also public bathhouses (sento) nearby — feel free to use whichever you prefer. If you go to a sento, please let us know the cost and we'll deduct it from your trip fee.

Sento reference info (nearest first):

  • Kame-no-yu — open until 20:00, ¥570, towel rental ¥30 (5 min walk from Shioiri Station)
  • Atari-yu — open until 21:00, ¥570
  • Sano Natural Hot Spring — last entry 23:00, ¥1,250
  • Tokiwa-yu — open until 22:00, ¥570

Hours and prices may have changed — please double-check.
